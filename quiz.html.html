Pressure Profile quiz

A quick reflective activity designed to help participants notice how pressure, urgency, and social context shape their decisions and responses.

How to use it

Partner up, answer instinctively, and move quickly. Then review the result and use the reflection prompts to notice what pressure brings forward in you.

    Which habits help you?
    Name the instincts that truly support your focus, peace, or courage.
    Which habits hurt you?
    Call out the moves that create confusion, delay repair, or raise the temperature.
    What changes when you slow down?
    Think about what gets better when you regulate before responding.
