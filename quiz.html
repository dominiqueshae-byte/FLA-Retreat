Lesson 1 • Emotional Communication

Big idea: how do emotions signal the brain and impact the way we communicate, react, and understand others?

Green lesson system

Slide deck

Lesson at a glance

Participants identify emotional and physical signals tied to communication, analyze miscommunication, practice clear communication through roleplay, and reflect on what improves understanding.

What matches this lesson

  • Same FULL rail as all other lessons
  • Same white slide layout and spacing
  • Same worksheet card system used below

Lesson 2 • Self-Awareness & Social Awareness

Big idea: how do our thoughts, emotions, and awareness of ourselves impact the way we behave and perceive others?

Burgundy lesson system

Slide deck

Lesson at a glance

Participants identify emotional and physical reactions to social situations, reflect on self-awareness and social awareness in real time, and observe the role of judgment, confidence, and vulnerability.

What matches this lesson

  • Shared white template with all other decks
  • Large sans-serif text for room readability
  • Worksheet and debrief language aligned to slide tone

Lesson 3 • Pressure Points

Big idea: how do stress, pressure, and competition affect the way we make decisions?

Gold lesson system

Slide deck

Lesson at a glance

Participants notice how pressure changes decision-making, compare reactive choices with thoughtful ones, and name their own stress-response tendencies using a quiz and challenge activity.

What matches this lesson

  • Extra slide for the second activity
  • Quiz and worksheet styled to match the lesson page
  • Same framework rail and spacing as the other two lessons
