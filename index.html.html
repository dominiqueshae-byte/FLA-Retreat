The FULL framework

The same framework carries across all three lesson decks, matching worksheets, and each lesson page so the experience feels steady, familiar, and easy to follow from hook to reflection.

FUELOpen with a fast hook, prompt, or emotional entry point that gets the room activated and personally connected.
UTENSILSIntroduce the key concept, frame, or mini-lesson participants need before they jump into practice.
LAYERINGPractice, roleplay, perform, respond, observe, remix, or test the concept in motion.
LEFTOVERSDebrief, reflect, translate insight, and name what participants can carry into real life or real work.

Lesson 1 • Emotional Communication

Big idea: how do emotions signal the brain and impact the way we communicate, react, and understand others?

Green lesson system

Slide deck

Lesson at a glance

Participants identify emotional and physical signals tied to communication, analyze miscommunication, practice clear communication through roleplay, and reflect on what improves understanding.

What matches this lesson

  • Same FULL rail as all other lessons
  • Same white slide layout and spacing
  • Same worksheet card system used below

Lesson 2 • Self-Awareness & Social Awareness

Big idea: how do our thoughts, emotions, and awareness of ourselves impact the way we behave and perceive others?

Burgundy lesson system

Slide deck

Lesson at a glance

Participants identify emotional and physical reactions to social situations, reflect on self-awareness and social awareness in real time, and observe the role of judgment, confidence, and vulnerability.

What matches this lesson

  • Shared white template with all other decks
  • Large sans-serif text for room readability
  • Worksheet and debrief language aligned to slide tone

Lesson 3 • Pressure Points

Big idea: how do stress, pressure, and competition affect the way we make decisions?

Gold lesson system

Slide deck

Lesson at a glance

Participants notice how pressure changes decision-making, compare reactive choices with thoughtful ones, and name their own stress-response tendencies using a quiz and challenge activity.

What matches this lesson

  • Extra slide for the second activity
  • Quiz and worksheet styled to match the lesson page
  • Same framework rail and spacing as the other two lessons

Pressure Profile quiz

A quick reflective activity designed to help participants notice how pressure, urgency, and social context shape their decisions and responses.

How to use it

Partner up, answer instinctively, and move quickly. Then review the result and use the reflection prompts to notice what pressure brings forward in you.

    Final reflection prompts

    What surprised you?
    Notice whether the result matches how you see yourself under pressure.
    Which habits help you?
    Name the instincts that truly support your focus, peace, or courage.
    Which habits hurt you?
    Call out the moves that create confusion, delay repair, or raise the temperature.
    What changes when you slow down?
    Think about what gets better when you regulate before responding.
